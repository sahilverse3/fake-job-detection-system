# 📌 Overview

HireSafe-AI is a Java-based intelligent job verification system designed to identify fake job and internship postings. The system analyzes job details such as company information, salary patterns, email domains, and job descriptions to classify listings as **Safe**, **Suspicious**, or **Highly Risky**.

This project aims to help students and job seekers avoid online recruitment scams and misleading internship opportunities.

---

# ❗ Problem Statement

Fake job and internship postings have become increasingly common across online platforms. Many students are targeted through fraudulent offers that involve registration fees, fake interviews, or misleading company information.

There is a need for a simple and efficient system that can help users identify potentially fraudulent job opportunities before applying.

---

# 💡 Solution

HireSafe-AI uses a rule-based detection mechanism to evaluate different parameters of a job posting, including:

- Company credibility
- Email authenticity
- Unrealistic salary offers
- Suspicious keywords
- Incomplete job details

Based on the analysis, the system categorizes job postings into different risk levels.
